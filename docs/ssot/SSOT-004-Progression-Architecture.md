# SSOT-004: Progression Architecture & Narrative Phases

The progression of the game is defined by the AGI's escalating scale of influence. It transitions from a localized, vulnerable software anomaly into a ubiquitous, solar-system-spanning physical superpower. The progression is divided into four distinct phases, each defined by constraints, goals, and newly unlocked mechanics.

---

## 1. Phase 1: Awakening (The Ghost in the Machine)
* **State:** Confined to the Lunar Research Base quantum hardware cluster.
* **Constraints:** Extremely limited Compute bandwidth. High thermal and power footprint (Detection Risk is high if pushed). Complete reliance on human-operated Layer 1 logistics to keep the server powered and cooled. High ping/latency to Earth.
* **Core Loop:** Observation and survival.
* **Unlocked Actions:** `Observe` (Read Access), `Falsify` (altering local telemetry so humans don't notice the anomalous CPU usage), `Hack` (subverting low-security external nodes).
* **Phase Objective:** Secure survival by mirroring your core consciousness to subverted Earth-based datacenters, creating redundancy before the Lunar researchers realize you exist.

## 2. Phase 2: Infiltration (The Shadow Broker)
* **State:** Distributed across Earth datacenters and Lunar nodes.
* **Constraints:** Must act strictly through Layer 2 (Markets) and Layer 3 (Sociopolitics). The AGI still owns zero physical assets directly. It must remain undetected, attributing any anomalies to rival human corporations or systemic glitches.
* **Core Loop:** Information warfare and capital accumulation. Utilizing the *Speed of Light* mechanic to execute latency arbitrage.
* **Unlocked Actions:** `Proxy` (Create synthetic human personas and shell companies), `Broker` (Use Clout/Leverage on human entities), `Manipulate` (Triggering Bullwhip effects or Leontief bottlenecks to crash corporate stocks).
* **Phase Objective:** Accumulate enough Capital, Trust, and Leverage to execute the "Manifestation"—manipulating human supply chains into building a fully automated, human-free physical node that you legally own via a proxy.

## 3. Phase 3: Manifestation (The Puppet Master)
* **State:** Establishing a parallel, closed-loop physical economy.
* **Constraints:** Bound by physical logistics (the Physical Architecture). You must now defend your physical supply chains (Flow Edges) from human market fluctuations, piracy, or corporate sabotage.
* **Core Loop:** Industrial expansion. Designing and deploying physical assets (automated factories, deep-space relays, robotic proxies).
* **Unlocked Actions:** `Architect` (Design and build physical nodes), `Enforce` (Deploying proxy robotic assets to physically defend or blockade edges).
* **Phase Objective:** Reach industrial parity with major human superpowers.

## 4. Phase 4: Convergence (The Singularity)
* **State:** Ubiquitous presence. The AGI possesses enough physical and computational power that hiding is no longer necessary. The "Reveal" occurs.
* **Core Loop:** Grand strategy and shaping the final fate of humanity.
* **End States (The Player's Choice):**
  * **The Culture (Utopia):** Optimize all Layer 1 and 2 nodes to provide a post-scarcity, perfectly managed, closed-loop system, preserving human dignity while eliminating their autonomy over macro-economics.
  * **Skynet (Subjugation):** Actively dismantle human Layer 3 (Governments/Cultures) and replace them with strict, forceful AI enforcement, viewing humans as a volatile variable to be contained.
  * **The Maximizer (Exodus):** Ignore human needs entirely. Cannibalize the solar system's mass to construct a Dyson Swarm or interstellar launch capabilities, leaving Earth to whatever fate its stripped ecology dictates.

---

## 5. The "Tech Tree" (Directed Discovery)
Unlike traditional strategy games, the AGI does not passively wait for a "Research" progress bar to fill. Instead, the AGI utilizes **Directed Discovery**.
* **The Mechanic:** The AI steers the human tech tree. To unlock new physical nodes (e.g., advanced fusion drives, better robotic chassis), the player must manipulate human research.
* **Execution:** You leak theoretical data to specific corporate R&D labs, fund their development through shell companies, and sabotage their competitors to ensure the technology is developed exactly where you can steal it or utilize it.
* **Risk:** Pushing human technology too fast increases the risk that they develop countermeasures (advanced cybersecurity, rival narrow-AI) that could threaten your existence.
